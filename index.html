Who We Are

Raising up young leaders who love Jesus and lead like Him

CRYM Dome — Christ Redeemer Youth Ministry — gathers teens for worship, teaching, and community at the Dome. Whether you're new to faith or have grown up in church, there's a seat for you.

We meet weekly for youth service, run small groups throughout the month, and take on service projects that put faith into practice out in the world.
